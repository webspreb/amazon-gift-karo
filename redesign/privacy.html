Privacy Policy

How we handle data, cookies, and your privacy on GiftKaro.

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

GiftKaro is a curated gift discovery platform. We help you find thoughtful gifts by connecting you with Amazon product pages via affiliate links. This privacy policy explains what data we collect (if any), how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

We don't collect personal data. GiftKaro is a static content site. We don't have user accounts, login systems, or databases that store your personal information.

  • No registration required. You can browse GiftKaro anonymously without creating an account.
  • No tracking. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other tracking tools that monitor your behavior across sites.
  • No cookies (beyond theme). We use localStorage to remember your theme preference (e.g., Diwali theme). This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Affiliate Disclosure

GiftKaro participates in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.in products.

When you click a "View on Amazon" link on our site and make a purchase on Amazon, we receive a small commission from Amazon at no extra cost to you. This commission helps us maintain and improve GiftKaro.

Every product link on GiftKaro is clearly marked with affiliate disclosures as required by Amazon's Operating Agreement and applicable advertising regulations.

Cookies & Local Storage

GiftKaro uses minimal browser storage:

  • localStorage for theme preference. When you select a theme (e.g., Diwali, Christmas), we store your preference in localStorage. This is device-local only — we never access or transmit this data.
  • No third-party cookies. We don't embed third-party scripts that set cookies (no analytics, no ads, no social media widgets).

Third-Party Services

GiftKaro uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts. We load Inter and JetBrains Mono fonts from Google Fonts. Google may collect anonymized font request data per their privacy policy.
  • Amazon. Product links redirect you to Amazon.in. Amazon's own privacy policy applies to your experience on their site.

Data Sharing

We don't share, sell, or trade your data with anyone because we don't collect it. The only data transfer that occurs is when you click an affiliate link and are redirected to Amazon — at which point Amazon's privacy policy governs your data.

Children's Privacy

GiftKaro is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we'll address it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how GiftKaro handles data, please contact us.
